## Finding

No gauge is over its threshold, nor near it: the highest are `product` and `payment`, 25 %. Of the three features coming, partial payments is the one that reaches beyond its bounded context: BR-5 of `payment` excludes a partial payment, and "Paid" in `invoice` is an amount paid in full, so the feature changes `payment`'s spec and `invoice`'s status model, read through the `billing-internal` contract. The architecture baseline names a partly paid status among what would make the form of `invoice`'s aggregate be ruled again (`docs/plans/architecture-baseline.md:74`). Invoice reminders meets "nothing is sent to the customer in this version" in `invoice`'s spec. Both are settled in the specs first, with `partition-spec`, before any plan. The VIP sign was caught by the specs before code: BR-32 held.
